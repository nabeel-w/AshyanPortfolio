# 07 · Storage & delivery cost estimate — Vercel Blob + YouTube (v2)

**Since v2 (2026-11-25, #27) films are YouTube links**, so the site hosts and streams no film at all. What remains on **Vercel Blob** is images (covers, stills, gallery, logos, the portrait) and the **3–6 s muted hover loops** on work cards. The old 200 GB library of masters is **out of scope**: masters stay in Google Drive (or the editor's own archive), and the site only ever holds what visitors see.

Prices are list prices from Vercel's pricing page (re-checked 2026-11-25). They change, so re-check before quoting a client. USD per month.

## Assumptions

**What's stored**

| Part | Size | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Images, logos, PDFs | ~5 GB | Hi-res covers and stills for ~40 projects, logos, the portrait (AVIF/WebP are generated by `next/image`, not stored) |
| Hover loops | ~0.2 GB | ~40 loops × ~4 MB (720p, 3–6 s, H.264) |
| Films | 0 | YouTube, unlisted or public |

**Traffic scenarios**

| | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visits / month | 3,000 | 15,000 | 60,000 |
| Image GB from Blob (≈ 4 MB/visit; worst case, before `next/image` caching) | 12 GB | 60 GB | 240 GB |
| Loop GB from Blob (≈ 2 loops × 3 MB per visit; loops aren't proxied by `next/image`) | 18 GB | 90 GB | 360 GB |

## Monthly cost (Vercel Blob, on-demand rates)

Storage $0.023/GB/mo after 1 GB; data transfer $0.05/GB after 10 GB.

| | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Storage (5.2 GB) | $0.10 | $0.10 | $0.10 |
| Transfer (images + loops − 10 GB free) | $1.00 | $7.00 | $29.50 |
| **Total** | **$1.10** | **$7.10** | **$29.60** |
| Films (YouTube) | $0 | $0 | $0 |

These are worst cases: optimised images are served from Vercel's image cache after the first request, so real image transfer from Blob is far lower. Loops are the main variable. Keeping them at ~2–4 MB (the admin warns past 8 MB) keeps the high scenario under ~$30. On a Pro plan, part of this may fall under the plan's included usage.

**Compared with v1 (Mux + Blob):** Mux storage alone was ~$7.20/mo for 40 h of film before any delivery overage, plus the same image costs. v2 removes the film line entirely.

**Not included**: site hosting (Vercel Pro seat, required for commercial sites), the Neon database plan, domain and email. Price those separately.

## Client billing with markup

Base = medium scenario ≈ **$7.10/mo**.

| Markup | Monthly price | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Cost (0%) | $7.10 | $85.20 |
| +30% | $9.23 | $110.76 |
| +50% | $10.65 | $127.80 |
| +100% | $14.20 | $170.40 |

Suggestions:
- Bundle storage with hosting and maintenance into one "media & hosting" line item and round up, instead of itemising pennies.
- Add a **usage clause**: the price covers up to ~10 GB stored and ~200 GB transferred per month; above that, bill at cost + the agreed markup.
- Re-check prices at each renewal.

## How to re-run these numbers

- **Blob** = max(0, GB stored − 1) × $0.023 + max(0, GB delivered − 10) × $0.05
- GB delivered ≈ visits × (image MB per visit + loop plays per visit × loop MB) / 1024
